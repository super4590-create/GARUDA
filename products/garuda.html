Honest Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers regarding what GARUDA can and cannot do.

Certification: To be confirmed. As a prototype currently in testing, formal regulatory standards testing has not yet been conducted. Mandatory safety testing will be completed prior to commercial availability.
SOS is prepared for preconfigured contacts when GPS and a communication path are available; this is a prototype. It does not contact public dispatchers directly and cannot guarantee injury prevention.
Pricing: not announced. Cost structure and retail pricing will be announced once production tooling and component supply contracts are finalized.
Availability: early-access survey only. GARUDA is in active prototype field testing with beta riders. You can take the rider survey to join the early-access list.
Our provisional design target triggers the sequence when tilt exceeds 50 degrees accompanied by severe vibration. These parameters are provisional and subject to ongoing validation with our beta testing cohort.
The device sounds an audible alarm for 30 seconds. A rider who has simply dropped the helmet or is uninjured can press the cancellation button to dismiss the alarm before any notification is prepared.
