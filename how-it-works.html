Architecture & Logic

The Incident Response Sequence

GARUDA pairs physical headgear with motion-aware electronics. When severe road dynamics occur, the system initiates a structured sequence designed around human confirmation before any notification is prepared.

Three-Stage Flow

Detect · Check · SOS

A continuous safety loop. Hover or focus any step to pause the visualization.

01 Design target

1. Detect

Onboard sensors evaluate acceleration and tilt angles. A tilt exceeding 50 degrees paired with severe vibration triggers the sequence.

PROVISIONAL DUAL-TRIGGER
02 Design target

2. Check

The device sounds an immediate alert. A 30-second cancellation window gives the rider full agency to cancel the alarm with one button press.

30-SECOND WINDOW
03 To be confirmed

3. SOS Workflow

If the rider does not cancel within 30 seconds, the device prepares a distress notification with GPS coordinates for designated contacts.

Honest Engineering Status

Current Testing Status

We believe in honest, grounded engineering communication. Hardware reliability is built through road hours, not marketing claims.

Prototype STATUS

Field Testing in Progress

Field testing with 5 beta riders is ongoing on real road conditions in Chhattisgarh. The focus of current testing is evaluating sensor thresholds, vibration filtering, and rider wearability under day-to-day conditions.

To be confirmed DATA DISCLOSURE

No Test Results Published Yet

No formal numerical test results, false-alarm rates, or accuracy figures have been published yet. Data from field evaluations will be compiled and disclosed only after structured validation cohorts conclude.
