1. Informational & Prototype Scope

This website is published by Angiras Industries, an unincorporated engineering initiative based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, pre-incubated at the NIT Raipur Incubation Centre.

All technical specifications, dimensions, thresholds, and performance targets published on this website are provisional engineering design targets. The GARUDA system is an engineering prototype in active evaluation with a closed cohort of 5 beta riders.

2. No Commercial Sales, Prices, or Orders

This website does not offer products for retail sale, does not list commercial prices, and does not accept orders, pre-payments, or deposits. Any commercial or fleet pilots are subject to separate formal agreements.

3. No Guarantee of Injury Prevention or Rescue

Accident detection hardware and SOS notification sequences rely on sensor dynamics, battery power, physical cellular connectivity, and GPS satellite reception.

Neither Angiras Industries nor the GARUDA system guarantees injury prevention, rescue dispatch, or survival in a collision. Riders must always wear a properly fitted helmet and obey traffic safety laws.

4. Browser Simulation Terms

The interactive simulation component on how-it-works.html is an illustrative demonstration only. It operates entirely within your browser sandbox, makes zero external network requests, does not access device sensors or GPS hardware, and transmits no real alerts.

5. External Rider Survey Portal

The rider feedback survey is hosted on a separate external portal (https://garuda-helmet.vercel.app/). Participation is voluntary. Data submitted on that site is governed under that portal's hosting terms.
