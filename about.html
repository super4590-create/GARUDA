Origin & Purpose

Built to Bring You Home

Angiras Industries is an engineering-driven initiative based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, pre-incubated at the NIT Raipur Incubation Centre. We develop rider-safety headgear hardware focused on accident detection and structured emergency confirmation.

Incubation

Pre-Incubated at NIT Raipur

Supported by the NIT Raipur Incubation Centre, our core hardware development and prototyping efforts are anchored in Chhattisgarh, drawing on academic mentorship and regional engineering talent.
