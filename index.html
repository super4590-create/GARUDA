Incident Response Sequence

Detect. Check. Prepare SOS.

A small, predictable sequence designed for moments when seconds matter. The rider retains complete control to cancel false alarms before any message is prepared.

01 Design target

1. Motion & Vibration Trigger

Sensors evaluate orientation and acceleration. Tilt exceeding 50 degrees accompanied by severe vibration triggers the sequence.

PROVISIONAL, PENDING VALIDATION
02 Design target

2. 30-Second Rider Check

The device sounds an alert. If the rider is unhurt or the helmet was simply dropped, a single button press cancels the flow immediately.

CANCELLATION WINDOW
03 To be confirmed

3. Emergency Workflow

If unanswered after 30 seconds, the device prepares a distress notification with GPS coordinates to preconfigured personal contacts.
