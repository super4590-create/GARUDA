Legal & Governance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Angiras Industries, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

1. Entity & Principles

Angiras Industries is an unincorporated engineering initiative based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, pre-incubated at the NIT Raipur Incubation Centre. We believe in minimal data footprint.

No Emergency Contacts or Telemetry Collected: This website does not collect, store, or transmit rider emergency contacts, real-time geolocation telemetry, or sensor log data.

Cookies & Analytics: This site uses zero third-party tracking cookies, advertising beacons, or invasive analytics unless explicitly enabled and disclosed in future updates.

2. On-Site Business & Pilot Enquiry Form

When commercial partners, fleet operators, or researchers submit our enquiry form on contact.html, we collect:

  • Full Name
  • Work Email Address
  • Organisation / Company Name
  • Enquiry Type (pilot, partnership, investment, media, other)
  • Message Content

Third-Party Form Processor: Form submissions are handled via a third-party form processing provider (such as Formspree or equivalent; endpoint to be confirmed upon deployment). Data is encrypted in transit and processed strictly to respond to your communication.

Retention Period: Commercial enquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to evaluate partnership viability; final retention period is To be confirmed.

3. External Rider Survey & Early-Access Data

Rider research and early-access registrations are conducted on an external survey portal hosted at:
https://garuda-helmet.vercel.app/

That survey collects respondent name, phone number, email address, and 15 questions regarding riding patterns and preferences.

Data Storage Location & Access: The specific cloud data store and authorization access policies for survey data are To be confirmed, pending completion of technical infrastructure review.

4. User Rights Under India's DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, data principals have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data provided to Angiras Industries.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke commercial communication consent at any time without penalty.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our data administrator directly at:
angirasindustriesoo7@gmail.com
