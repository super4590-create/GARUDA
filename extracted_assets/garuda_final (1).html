Beyond ordinary riding gear

Protection is physical.
Support can be smarter.

GARUDA brings together a helmet shell, compact sensors and a simple emergency sequence—so a suspected crash can prompt a check instead of silence.

Impact-aware sensing

IMU tilt data and vibration sensing work together to identify movement patterns that may indicate a crash.

GPS location support

GPS coordinates can be included in the configured emergency message when a location fix and communication path are available.

Rider response check

A 30-second beep window gives the rider an opportunity to cancel the alert if they are okay and able to respond.

SOS workflow

If no cancellation is received, the system is designed to proceed with an SOS to preconfigured family and rescue contacts.

The hardware inside

Built from purposeful parts.

A compact set of sensing, connectivity and power components supports the helmet's intended detection and alert flow.

Always verify product specifications and applicable helmet certification before making the product available to riders. Electronics complement a properly fitted protective helmet; they do not replace it.

Motion sensing
MPU6050

Accelerometer and gyroscope data for motion and tilt.

Vibration
SW-420

Vibration trigger input for the detection workflow.

Location
NEO-6M GPS

Satellite-derived coordinates when a fix is available.

Connectivity
HC-05

Bluetooth communication with a paired device.

Power
1200mAh battery

Designed around an up-to-8-hour runtime target.

Charging
USB Type-C

Convenient charging connection.

Helmet shell
ABS · ISI specification

Confirm the exact approved model and certification marking.

Weight
Approx. 1450 g

Target product specification; verify on the finished unit.
